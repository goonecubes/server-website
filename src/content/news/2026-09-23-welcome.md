The lights are on and the elevators are running. **Skyline Tower** is a community-run revival of GMod Tower, and we're working toward opening the doors to everyone.

### What's already in

- The full **Lobby 2** experience: condos you can decorate and keep, stores, the theater, arcade, casino, nightclub, pool and beach.
- **Condos with CondOS**: music, lights, skybox views, guest lists and a vault that remembers your stuff.
- **Lobby events** every 15 to 25 minutes, from Balloon Pop to Chainsaw Battle.
- Classic gamemodes including Ball Race, Virus, PVP Battle, Ultimate Chimera Hunt, Zombie Massacre and Minigolf.

### What's next

We're finishing security work and a closed beta before we open to the public. Keep an eye on this page for updates.

See you in the tower!
